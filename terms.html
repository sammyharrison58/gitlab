Terms of Service

Last updated: 2 March 2026

These draft terms describe the intended use of Rent Master, a service for discovering rental listings and organizing landlord, tenant and rent-reference information. They must be reviewed and completed by the service operator and qualified counsel before accepting customers.

Accounts and eligibility

Users must provide accurate account information, keep access credentials confidential and use an email address they control. Landlords are responsible for having authority to list and manage each property. Account access may depend on email verification and an active invitation or tenancy.

Listings and rental decisions

Listing information is supplied by landlords. Rent Master does not represent that a property, landlord, price, description, availability or condition has been independently verified. Tenants should inspect a property and independently confirm the landlord, lease, payment recipient and all terms before paying a deposit or entering an agreement.

Rent and M-Pesa records

Rent is paid directly by tenants to the landlord using the landlord’s stated payment instructions. Rent Master does not receive, hold, safeguard, transfer or settle rent payments. A submitted receipt reference is a record for review and is not proof that a payment cleared. Landlords are responsible for verifying receipts against their payment statements.

Acceptable use

Users must not submit false payment references, misrepresent ownership or authority, misuse personal information, attempt to access another account, or interfere with the operation or security of the service.

Service availability, fees and liability

Service levels, subscription fees, cancellation terms, dispute procedures, governing law, liability limits and operator contact details have not yet been finalized. These terms must not be used as final customer terms until those items are completed and reviewed.

Contact and operator information

The legal name, registered address and customer-support contact for the Rent Master operator must be added before public launch.
