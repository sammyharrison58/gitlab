Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 March 2026

This draft describes information the production Rent Master application is designed to handle. The operator must confirm the legal entity, purposes, lawful bases, retention periods, international transfers, user rights and contact details before launch.

Information processed

Account name, email address and role; property addresses, rent amounts, descriptions and landlord-provided M-Pesa payment instructions; landlord-tenant assignments; submitted M-Pesa receipt references and review status; account notices; and technical records needed to secure and operate the service.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, display public listings, share property and payment instructions with assigned tenants, organize rent records, deliver transactional email notices, protect accounts and respond to support requests.

Who receives information

Information is shared only as required to provide the service: property information with relevant account users, account and transactional-email data with the configured hosting, authentication, database and email providers, and information with authorities when legally required. The operator must document provider locations and applicable transfer safeguards.

Payments

Rent is transferred directly from tenant to landlord outside Rent Master. The service stores the landlord-provided payment instructions and tenant-submitted receipt reference. Do not enter M-Pesa PINs, card numbers, passwords or other payment credentials in a receipt-reference field.

Security and retention

Production data is intended to be protected by authenticated accounts and database access policies. The operator must configure, monitor and test the production project, backups, incident response, access controls and deletion/retention schedule. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your privacy requests

The operator’s legal name and privacy contact must be added before launch. The operator must establish a process for access, correction, deletion, objection and other applicable requests before collecting customer information.
