The rental journey

Clear steps. Better-organized rentals.

Rent Master gives landlords and tenants a shared place for property details, access and rent-reference records.

01

Landlord lists a home

A landlord registers property details, chooses whether to publish a listing and adds private M-Pesa payment instructions.

02

Tenant gets invited

The landlord registers a tenant’s email. The tenant creates an account with that same verified email to access their assigned rental.

03

Rent is paid directly

The tenant pays the landlord outside Rent Master using the stated Till or Paybill, then submits the M-Pesa reference.

04

Landlord checks the receipt

The landlord matches the reference against their M-Pesa statement and confirms or rejects it. The tenant receives an account notice and email.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does Rent Master process rent payments?

No. Rent is paid directly to the landlord. Rent Master stores the landlord’s instructions and records a tenant-submitted reference; it does not hold or transfer funds.

When is a payment marked confirmed?

Only after the landlord checks the reference against their M-Pesa statement and manually updates its status.

Why can’t I see the full property address?

Public listings show the area. Full address and payment instructions are available only to an assigned tenant and the landlord.

What should I check before renting?

Verify the property, landlord authority, availability, payment recipient and lease terms independently before sending money or signing an agreement.
